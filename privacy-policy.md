# Privacy Policy — IoT Lab Portal

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction
IoT Lab Portal is a laboratory equipment management system used to manage student registration, equipment inventory, RFID-based equipment issuing and returns, and related notifications.

## 2. Information We Collect
The system may collect student names, registration numbers, email addresses, department and course details, RFID card identifiers, and equipment issue and return records.

## 3. How We Use Information
Information is used only for student verification, laboratory equipment tracking, inventory management, and sending relevant system notifications.

## 4. Information Sharing
We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is accessible only to authorized laboratory staff for legitimate laboratory administration.

## 5. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect stored information and restrict access to authorized users.

## 6. Data Retention
Information is retained as required for laboratory administration and institutional record-keeping.

## 7. Contact
For privacy-related questions, contact the laboratory administrator through the department's official contact channel.
